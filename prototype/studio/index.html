AN HONEST STARTING POINT

A prototype, with clear boundaries.

This build detects general luminance flashes. It does not certify WCAG compliance, analyze saturated-red flashes, decode animated images, or guarantee pre-render blocking. Browser sampling can miss changes. The area estimate is an engineering heuristic. Cross-origin or unreadable pixels stay guarded.
